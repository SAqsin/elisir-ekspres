Privacy policy (English)

This policy covers Action Lab's game "Əlişir Ekspres – Kuryer" (Android and iOS) and the game's social media accounts. Last updated 8 November 2026.

What we do not collect

The game has no accounts, sign-up or login. We do not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, location or contacts. The game has no server of its own.

What stays on your device

Game progress, garage, score and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows Google AdMob ads. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier, IP address and general device information. In the EEA and the UK the game first asks for consent with Google's consent form; on iOS, tracking permission is asked by Apple's prompt. You can change these choices in your device settings at any time. See how Google uses information from ads and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. They process your payment details; we never see them. We only receive the purchase confirmation so the item unlocks in the game.

Social media

We use our own tool to upload our own videos to the game's YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It signs in only to our own accounts and posts only our own content. It has no access to other users' data and does not sell or share any data.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with its date. Questions: akshins.lab@gmail.com.
